# 🔗 Backlink Outreach Strategy & Templates

**Doel**: Bouw links van relevante websites naar jouw site via strategic outreach.

## 📋 Backlink Outreach Strategy

### **Waar Links Vandaan Halen?**

1. **Tech Blogs & Publications**
   - Dev.to, Medium, Hashnode
   - Dutch tech sites: Tweakers.net, Programmeren.nl
   - Focus: Guest posts & mentions

2. **Startup & Business Networks**
   - Startup.com, AngelList, LinkedIn
   - Local business directories (Netherlands)
   - Industry-specific communities

3. **Resource Lists & Directories**
   - "Best SaaS Tools" lists
   - "Web Development Companies in Netherlands"
   - Tech directories

4. **Partnerships & Collaborations**
   - Other dev agencies (non-competing)
   - Tool companies (if you use them)
   - Educational platforms

## 📊 Link Building Checklist

For each outreach, track:

```
[ ] Research target websites (check DA/authority)
[ ] Identify contact person
[ ] Customize pitch (NOT generic mass email)
[ ] Include clear value proposition
[ ] Send follow-up if no response (1-2 weeks)
[ ] Log result (yes/no/pending)
[ ] Update backlink tracking
```

## 💡 Backlink Best Practices

### ❌ DON'T DO (Black Hat)

- Buy backlinks
- Link farms or PBN (Private Blog Networks)
- Automated comment link insertion
- Keyword-heavy anchor text spam
- Reciprocal linking schemes

### ✅ DO THIS (White Hat)

- Create genuinely valuable content
- Build relationships with other websites
- Guest posts with natural links
- Reach out to relevant communities
- Get mentions & citations
- Participate in interviews/podcasts

## 📈 Outreach Timeline

**Month 1-2: Foundation**
- Identify 20-30 target websites
- Create 1-2 pieces of content worth linking to
- Send 5-10 personalized pitches

**Month 2-3: Momentum**
- Publish guest posts
- Get featured in directories
- Send 10-15 more pitches

**Month 3-6: Scaling**
- Content marketing continues
- Monitor backlinks
- 1-2 new quality links per week = 8-10 per month

**Goal**: 20-30 quality backlinks within 6 months
